This page describes the technical and organizational measures Spifex uses to protect the website, services and customer workspace data. It supports the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/data_processing_agreement) and is reviewed as the platform evolves.

No system is completely secure. These measures are designed to reduce risk, detect issues and support a controlled response when something goes wrong.

## Access control

- **Unique users:** Access to customer workspaces requires an authenticated user account. Shared credentials are not supported.
- **Role-based access:** Owners, administrators and members receive permissions based on their role and workspace scope. Privileged actions are restricted.
- **Least privilege:** Personnel access to production systems is limited to those who need it for operations, support or security.
- **Authentication:** Passwords, session controls and optional multi-factor authentication protect account access. Security-sensitive actions require re-authentication or verification where appropriate.
- **Access reviews:** Access is reviewed when roles change and when personnel leave.
- **Workspace isolation:** Application queries are scoped to the authenticated user’s workspace or organization. Tenant boundaries are enforced server-side.

## Encryption

- Data in transit is encrypted using TLS.
- Data at rest is encrypted by the infrastructure providers used to host the application and databases.
- Secrets and credentials are stored in managed configuration or secret stores rather than in source code.
- Backups are protected with the same access and encryption controls as production data.

## Logging and monitoring

- Application and security events are logged to support troubleshooting, abuse detection and audit needs.
- Administrative actions and changes affecting access or financial records are designed to retain an audit trail.
- Logs are monitored for errors, unusual access patterns and service health.
- Access to logs is restricted and retained for a defined period described in the [Data Retention Policy](/legal/data_retention).

## Application security

- Security is considered during design and code review.
- Dependencies are monitored and updated to address known vulnerabilities.
- Changes are tested before release, with separate development and production environments.
- Database migrations and financial operations are designed to be controlled and auditable.
- Error messages avoid exposing sensitive internal details.

## Backups and resilience

- Production data is backed up according to the infrastructure provider’s schedule and retention settings.
- Backups are tested periodically for restoreability.
- The service is designed for availability through managed infrastructure and monitoring.
- A business continuity and disaster recovery process exists for significant outages.

## Incident response

- Security incidents are triaged, contained and investigated.
- A documented incident response process assigns roles for communication, technical response and post-incident review.
- Customers are notified without undue delay when a personal data breach affects their data, as required by the DPA and applicable law.
- Post-incident reviews identify root causes and corrective actions.

## Vendor and subprocessor management

- Subprocessors are assessed before onboarding for security and data protection suitability.
- Data protection terms are required where personal data is processed.
- The current categories are described on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.
- Vendor access is limited to the scope needed to provide the service.

## Personnel and organization

- Personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations.
- Security and privacy responsibilities are assigned.
- Access to customer data is limited to support, engineering and security personnel who need it.
- Security awareness is part of onboarding and ongoing practice.

## Physical and infrastructure security

Spifex relies on managed infrastructure providers for physical data center security, including access controls, environmental protections and hardware lifecycle management. We select providers that maintain industry-standard security programs and make appropriate contractual commitments.

## AI connections

AI connections are designed to be read-only:

- The connector requests a limited scope such as `cashflow:read`.
- The assistant cannot create, edit, delete, settle or transfer records through the connection.
- Access is authorized per user and can be revoked.
- Workspace boundaries apply to connected data.
- Customer data is not used to train external AI models by Spifex.

See the [AI Transparency Notice](/legal/ai_transparency) for more detail.

## Customer responsibilities

Security is shared. Customers are responsible for:

- managing user access and roles;
- protecting credentials and enabling MFA where available;
- reviewing workspace permissions;
- configuring retention and exports appropriately; and
- notifying Spifex promptly of suspected unauthorized access.

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability or suspect unauthorized access, contact [legal@spifex.com](mailto:legal@spifex.com). Please include enough detail to reproduce the issue and avoid accessing data that does not belong to you.
