## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Spifex (“Spifex”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you:

- visit Spifex websites and marketing pages;
- create or manage a Spifex account or workspace;
- use Spifex products and services;
- contact us for sales, support, partnerships or feedback; or
- interact with our communications or campaigns.

Spifex provides financial operations software. Depending on the workspace and features used, Spifex may help users organize information related to cash flow, banking visibility, settlements, ledger structure, projects, departments, counterparties, inventory records, reporting and AI connections. Spifex is not a bank, payment institution, accounting firm, tax advisor or law firm.

## Business workspaces and our role

This Policy applies to personal data processed by Spifex in connection with the Website and Services.

For business workspaces, customers may enter or upload data about their own users, employees, contractors, customers, suppliers or other individuals (“Workspace Data”). In those cases, the customer usually decides what Workspace Data to submit and how it is used in their operations. Spifex processes Workspace Data to provide and secure the Services, subject to the applicable agreement, product configuration and law.

Our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/data_processing_agreement) describes the processor obligations that apply to Workspace Data.

## Personal data we collect

The data we collect depends on how you interact with Spifex.

### Information you provide

We may collect:

- account details, such as name, email address, company name, role and authentication-related information;
- profile and workspace settings;
- communications, such as support requests, sales inquiries and feedback;
- billing or subscription information, where applicable, which may be handled by payment providers; and
- content you choose to submit through forms, uploads or product features.

### Workspace Data

If you use Spifex products, Workspace Data may include financial and operational records entered by authorized users. Depending on the modules used, this can include:

- cash flow plans, inflows, outflows and related notes;
- bank account references, transaction records, statement data and matching information;
- settlement, payment and approval-related records, where those features are used;
- ledger categories, classifications and reporting structures;
- projects, departments, counterparties and inventory records; and
- audit or activity information needed to operate and secure the workspace.

You should only submit Workspace Data that you are authorized to use and that is appropriate for the Services.

### Technical and usage data

When you use the Website or Services, we may collect limited technical information, such as:

- IP address, browser type, device information and approximate location derived from network data;
- pages viewed, actions taken, timestamps and referral information;
- diagnostic, security and performance logs; and
- cookie or local storage data described in our [Cookie Notice](/legal/cookie_notice).

### Marketing and attribution data

If you consent to marketing technologies, or where otherwise permitted, we may collect campaign and attribution information, such as UTM parameters, ad click identifiers, landing page, referrer and conversion events. See the [Cookie Notice](/legal/cookie_notice) for details.

## How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate and maintain the Website and Services;
- create and manage accounts, workspaces and user access;
- process subscriptions, billing or related requests where applicable;
- respond to inquiries and provide support;
- monitor security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot issues;
- improve product performance, usability and reliability;
- measure marketing performance when consent or another legal basis applies;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms; and
- protect the rights, safety and integrity of Spifex, our users and others.

## Legal bases

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK data protection law or similar laws apply, we rely on legal bases such as:

- **Contract**: to provide requested Services and manage accounts.
- **Legitimate interests**: to secure, maintain and improve the Services, prevent abuse, and communicate about relevant business services, where appropriate.
- **Consent**: for non-essential cookies or marketing technologies where required.
- **Legal obligation**: to comply with applicable laws, requests or recordkeeping duties.

In Brazil, where the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) applies, Spifex handles personal data in accordance with applicable LGPD legal bases and rights.

We may share personal data with:

- **Service providers** that help us host infrastructure, provide security, deliver communications, process payments, analyze product usage or measure marketing performance. Our current subprocessor categories are described in the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page;

- **Professional advisors**, such as legal, accounting or compliance advisors, where necessary;

## Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, meet contractual commitments, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of data, the workspace configuration, the nature of the relationship and applicable law. Workspace administrators may also control certain data within their workspaces. Our [Data Retention Policy](/legal/data_retention) provides the current retention schedule.

## Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data. These include access controls, encryption, tenant isolation, logging, backups and incident response. The current measures are described in [Security and Technical Organisational Measures](/legal/security_measures).

No system or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and for managing access within your workspace.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- access personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request deletion;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- request portability;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise rights related to a Spifex workspace, you may also need to contact your workspace administrator, who may control the relevant Workspace Data.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in the country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement occurred. You can find your national authority through the European Data Protection Board. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first at [privacy@spifex.com](mailto:privacy@spifex.com).

## Automated decision-making and AI

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about individuals without human involvement.

Optional AI connections are read-only and are intended for analysis and navigation. They do not make automated decisions about individuals and cannot create, edit, settle or transfer records. See the [AI Transparency Notice](/legal/ai_transparency) for details.
