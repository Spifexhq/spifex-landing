## Overview

This Cookie Notice explains how Spifex uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies when you visit `www.spifex.com` and related Spifex websites (the “Website”).

Some technologies are necessary for the Website to work. Others, such as marketing measurement technologies, are used only when you give consent through the cookie banner, where required.

This Notice should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). If anything here is unclear, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

## Technologies we use

When we refer to “cookies and similar technologies,” we mean:

- **Cookies**: small text files stored by your browser.
- **Local storage**: browser storage used to remember choices or limited website data.
- **Pixels and scripts**: code that helps measure visits, conversions or website performance when enabled.

## Strictly necessary and preference technologies

These technologies support core Website functions. They do not require marketing consent.

| Technology | Type | Purpose | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `spifex_locale` | Cookie | Remembers your language preference. | Session or until updated |
| `spifex_country` | Cookie | Helps display region-appropriate content, such as currency context. | Session or until updated |
| `spx_cookie_consent` | Cookie | Stores your cookie choice (accepted or rejected for marketing technologies). | Up to 180 days |
| `spx_cookie_consent_v1` | Local storage | Stores the same cookie choice as a fallback when storage is available. | Until cleared or changed |

## Analytics and attribution technologies

The Website may store limited attribution information to understand how visitors reached the site, such as campaign parameters, landing page, referrer and timestamps. This information can be stored in local storage keys such as:

- `spx_attribution_first`
- `spx_attribution_session`

Attribution data may include values such as `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, `fbclid` or `gclid`, together with landing page and referrer information.

## Marketing technologies

Marketing technologies are used only after you choose to allow them through the cookie banner.

If you consent, Spifex may load Meta (Facebook) measurement tools, such as the Meta Pixel, and may send conversion events through a server-side endpoint (`/api/meta-events`) to measure campaign performance and improve marketing relevance.

When Meta technologies are enabled, Meta may set or read cookies and identifiers, such as `_fbp` and `_fbc`, subject to Meta’s own policies and your browser settings.

Spifex does not load Google Analytics on this Website based on the current implementation.

## Your choices

You can manage non-essential technologies in these ways:

- **Cookie banner**: choose whether to allow marketing technologies when the banner appears.
- **Browser settings**: block, delete or limit cookies and site data.
- **Device and platform controls**: adjust advertising or tracking preferences provided by your browser, operating system or advertising platforms.

If you reject marketing technologies, the Website should still work, but some marketing measurement and campaign attribution features may be limited.

If you delete cookies or local storage, or use a different browser/device, you may need to make your choice again.

## Third-party services

Some features may involve third-party providers, such as hosting, analytics/measurement providers, payment providers or embedded content. Those providers may process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

When marketing consent is given, Meta is the main third-party marketing provider currently used by this Website. Review Meta’s privacy information for details about how Meta processes data.

## Updates to this Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice when our Website, technologies or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
