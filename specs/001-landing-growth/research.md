# Research: Search intent and positioning

**Feature**: `001-landing-growth` · **Date**: 2026-11-09

## Product facts used

- Two product categories: Personal and Business.
- Both have Free, Starter and Pro plans.
- Business Starter/Pro: EUR 15/27, USD 12/30, BRL 50/150 per month.
- Personal Starter/Pro: EUR 9.99/14.99, USD 7.99/12.99, BRL 39.99/109.99.
- Business plan limits: 1/2/6 workspaces; 1,000/10,000/unlimited entries; 2/10/unlimited banks; 1/5/50 members; 2/50/unlimited projects; 10/100/unlimited ledger accounts; 2/20/unlimited statement imports.
- Personal plan limits: 1/2/6 workspaces; 250/5,000/50,000 transactions per month; 2/10/50 bank accounts; 3/25/250 budgets and goals.
- Implemented capabilities: cash-flow planning, settlements, transfers, bank accounts, statement imports, ledger, projects, departments, CRM, inventory, KPIs, roles and read-only AI connections.

## Content rules

- Do not publish invented customer counts, logos, testimonials or benchmark percentages.
- Pricing is quoted from the existing catalog and regional currency logic.
- Use one clear H1 per page and avoid repeating the same commercial page for both audiences.
- Keep Personal and Business pages differentiated by workflow, language, plan limits and CTAs.
- Add future guides only where they answer a real implementation question.
